# Transcriptomic efficiency shapes growth-defense trade-offs in alpine desert plants under climate change

This repository contains the R scripts and source data used to reproduce the main statistical analyses and figures associated with the manuscript:

**Transcriptomic efficiency shapes growth-defense trade-offs in alpine desert plants under climate change**

The study examines physiological and transcriptomic responses of two co-dominant alpine desert shrubs, *Ajania tibetica* and *Krascheninnikovia ceratoides* (referred to as *Ceratoides compacta* in the associated manuscript), to experimental warming and watering in the alpine desert of Xizang, China.
